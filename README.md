# OneForAll

All-in-one companion addon for **WoW Forever**. No other addons required.

- **Gear Planner** – click a slot of your character to see upgrades from dungeons, quests, reputation and crafting that your class can use, with the gain in % for your talent spec (also in item tooltips), a wishlist, quest chains and the boss that drops an item, a source filter and your own stat weights; enchants, armor kits and scopes that fit each item and who can make them
- **Armory** – item sets for your class (raid, dungeon, PvP, crafted, world; buttons for the main tiers) with the pieces you own, set bonuses and where they drop; reputation rewards by standing
- **Dungeons & Raids** – dungeon maps with boss portraits (M inside a dungeon), bosses with levels, loot with drop chances, quests with quest chains and a map button for every quest giver and turn-in, dungeon keys and raid attunements; the new WoW Forever dungeons included
- **Library Books** – which books you have, still need, and where to find them
- **Professions** – a Skill-Up Helper with the cheapest way to your target skill and a step-by-step guide, a Profit page with the recipes worth crafting (both with Auctionator prices), a Crafting List that checks your bags, bank and other characters for the materials and buys missing ones at a merchant with one click, Merchant's Favor recipes, who in your guild (or on your other characters) knows a recipe, the nearest trainer of every profession
- **Talent planner** – plan your talents with two saved presets per class, import builds from talentsforever.com / wowforevertalents.com and export your own; trainer window (also a tab in the spellbook) with the spells you can learn next, weapon skills and your class quests with progress
- **Hunter pets** – where to tame which family, which abilities a pet can learn
- **Map Landmarks** – dungeon and raid entrances, flight masters, zeppelins, ships, the Deeprun Tram and Library Books on the world map; click an entrance to open that dungeon
- **Quality of Life** – optional helpers in groups with a search (an info bar at the top of the screen, guild members with OneForAll on the world map, auto repair, sell junk with your own junk list, hide the chat, quest XP on the experience bar, item compare with Shift, tooltip at the mouse, minimap button collector, square minimap, sparkle on herbs and ore, brighter class colors, low spell ranks on your action bars, popups and alerts and more); a welcome setup switches them on page by page
- **Look** – one gold modern style that can be made see-through, and a logo in five colors
- **Languages** – English, German, French and Spanish (Settings > General > Language); in a German, French or Spanish game client quest, NPC, item and zone names come in that language too

## Download

Get the latest release on **CurseForge** and install it with the CurseForge app, or copy the `OneForAll` folder into the `Interface/AddOns` folder of your WoW Forever installation.

Open the addon with `/ofa` or the minimap button. All commands are listed in `OneForAll/README.txt`.

## Bug reports and ideas

Please post bugs and ideas in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/bQhjSpdA3S) (#bug-reports or #suggestions) with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot.
The bug button in the addon's title bar shows the link and your recent errors to copy.
`/ofa where` prints your current map and coordinates, which helps with wrong map pins.
