OneForAll - dungeon data sources
===============================================================================
Where the dungeon data in Data\Dungeons, Data\DungeonQuestChains.lua,
Data\DungeonKeys.lua, Data\DungeonDropChance.lua and Data\BossInfo.lua comes
from. Newest sections at the bottom. Facts (IDs, levels, coordinates, drop
tables) come from the sources named below; all descriptions and notes are
written for OneForAll.

General sources
- Quests, quest givers, turn-ins, objectives, prerequisites, NPC spawns:
  QuestieDB Forever (https://github.com/Questie/questiedb, GPL-3, see
  THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt), cross-checked with Wowhead Forever quest pages.
- Quest rewards and XP: wowforevertalents.com quest pages and Wowhead Forever.
- Boss and trash loot: foreverchanges.pro dungeon pages and wowtbc.gg loot
  tables; item names, slots and qualities from the Wowhead tooltip API
  (Forever data, Classic data as fallback).
- Boss portraits (display IDs) and level ranges: foreverchanges.pro.
- Classic drop chances: foreverchanges.pro (Wowhead Classic data).


4.4.0 dungeons (Stockade, Gnomeregan, SM Graveyard, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran), checked 30 September 2026
===============================================================================
- Level ranges: https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dungeons/ (new
  dungeons match https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons-overview-locations-details).
- Quests, quest givers, coordinates and rewards: Wowhead Forever quest pages
  (https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=<id>).
- Boss loot: Wowhead Forever NPC pages, boss-specific drops only (green or better,
  at least 3 % drop chance, Season of Discovery items excluded). Classic items that
  are missing from the Forever tables (e.g. Thermaplugg's Left Arm) are not listed.
- Dark Iron Ambassador has no Forever drop table yet.
- Excavation Site and City of Dalaran: boss NPC IDs from Wowhead Forever; no loot
  or quests are published. Mana Elemental has no confirmed Forever NPC ID.
- Prerequisites/breadcrumbs: checked against the QuestieDB Forever data as a reference
  (github.com/Questie/questiedb), cross-checked with Wowhead.
- Entrance coordinates for the new dungeons are not verified in game yet
  (the Map button tooltip says so). City of Dalaran has no entrance position yet.


Hunter pet data (Data/HunterPets.lua), updated 3 October 2026
===============================================================================
- Pet families, abilities, ranks, training points and teaching beasts:
  https://www.wow-petopia.com/forever/ (family pages and abilities.php).
  Spawn coordinates for the Map buttons from QuestieDB (Forever data).
- Forever-only abilities (Dismember, Dust Cloud, Lava Breath, Mine!, Pinch,
  Savage Rend, Swipe, Tendon Rip, Trickster's Dance, Web, Demoralizing Screech)
  have no known training source on Petopia yet. Core Hounds and Foxes: tameable
  beasts not confirmed yet.
- Known gaps: no known teacher for Charge rank 4 and Lightning Breath rank 1;
  Growl listed with 0 training points (single source).


Profession recipes (Data/Professions/*.lua), extracted 5 October 2026
===============================================================================
- Recipes, skill colors, reagents, crafted items and recipe items from the WoW
  Forever client database, build 1.60.1.70205 (DB2 exports from wago.tools:
  SkillLineAbility, SpellEffect, SpellReagents, ItemSparse, ItemEffect,
  ItemXItemEffect, TradeSkillCategory).
- Recipe sources (vendors, drops, quest rewards) and coordinates from QuestieDB
  (Forever data). Season of Discovery leftovers in the client data are excluded.

2026-10-09 Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery: Library, recipe drops
- Razorfen Kraul and Scarlet Monastery: Library: quests, levels, start/end NPCs,
  rewards and XP from Wowhead Forever quest pages; quest chains, NPC spawns and
  boss levels from QuestieDB Forever; boss and trash loot from wowtbc.gg loot
  tables (trash: rare and better only).
- Recipe drops of dungeon bosses (Data\Professions\BossDrops.lua) and the
  drop / vendor sources of recipes that had none: Wowhead Forever item pages
  ("Dropped by" / "Sold by"). Forever recipes come from a shared low-rate pool,
  so bosses can drop recipes that are not listed yet.


2026-10-12 Ten more dungeons (Armory to Sunken Temple)
===============================================================================
Scarlet Monastery: Armory and Cathedral, The Drowned City, Razorfen Downs,
Krol'dok Stronghold, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, Alcaz Prison and
The Temple of Atal'Hakkar.
- Level ranges: foreverchanges.pro dungeon pages (Blizzard forum post on the
  new dungeon level ranges for the new dungeons).
- Quests, quest levels, start/end NPCs and their coordinates, prerequisite
  chains and Classic reward items: QuestieDB Forever data
  (github.com/Questie/questiedb), checked against the Wowhead Forever dungeon
  quest guide (wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons/every-dungeon-quest-location).
- Quests seen on the beta (rewards incl. new Forever items, XP, money):
  wowforevertalents.com quest pages (beta quest cache). Quests not seen on
  the beta yet show their Classic rewards; Forever may add new reward choices.
- XP fallbacks: beta value where recorded, otherwise the Classic value when
  the Forever quest level is unchanged.
- Boss loot (Classic drop tables, no drop chances): wowtbc.gg Forever loot
  tables; foreverchanges.pro for bosses wowtbc.gg does not list (Sunken
  Temple, Zul'Farrak event bosses, Uldaman Obsidian Sentinel, Maraudon Veng
  and Maraudos) and for Zul'Farrak / Sunken Temple trash. Item slots and
  qualities: Wowhead Forever tooltips (Classic tooltips when Forever has no
  entry yet). Trash: rare and better only.
- Boss levels: QuestieDB Forever NPC data.
- The Drowned City: boss names and NPC IDs from the Wowhead Forever database
  (placeholder titles "Staging Area 1-6"), Zin'aka from the BlizzCon demo
  (Warcraft Tavern via foreverchanges.pro). Three Horde quests from the beta
  quest cache (wowforevertalents.com). No loot recorded yet.
- Krol'dok Stronghold and Alcaz Prison: location and story only (Blizzard at
  BlizzCon / PAX Australia via foreverchanges.pro and wowforevertalents.com).
  No bosses, quests or loot are known yet.
- Entrance coordinates for the Classic dungeons: QuestieDB dungeon entrances;
  not verified in game yet.


2026-10-12 Last ten dungeons (Blackrock Depths to Shaper's Terrace)
===============================================================================
Blackrock Depths, Lower and Upper Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul East / West /
North, Scholomance, Stratholme (one entry for both gates), Blackmaw Hold and
Shaper's Terrace.
- Same sources and rules as the previous ten dungeons (QuestieDB Forever,
  Wowhead Forever dungeon quest guide, wowtbc.gg, foreverchanges.pro).
- Lower and Upper Blackrock Spire loot: foreverchanges.pro (Classic drop
  tables); wowtbc.gg has no tables for them. Upper Blackrock Spire, Scholomance
  and Stratholme quests are not in the Wowhead guide; they are taken from
  QuestieDB Forever (dungeon zone and quest text).
- Scholomance minor bosses (Malicia, Krastinov, Polkelt, Ravenian, the Barovs)
  and the Dire Maul North guards: foreverchanges.pro loot tables.
- Boss portraits: creature display IDs from foreverchanges.pro boss pictures
  (also added for the previous ten dungeons).
- Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace: location and story only.
- Entrance coordinates: QuestieDB dungeon entrances (Blackrock Mountain via
  Searing Gorge); not verified in game yet.


2026-10-12 Dungeon keys (Data\DungeonKeys.lua)
===============================================================================
- Key items, quest chains and NPC positions: QuestieDB Forever (item, quest
  and NPC data); key drops (Crescent Key, Workshop Key, Key to the City,
  Prison Cell Key) from the QuestieDB drop data and foreverchanges.pro loot
  tables. The Scarlet Key and Skeleton Key are still needed in Forever
  according to the Wowhead Forever dungeon quest guide; the others follow
  Classic and are not verified on the beta yet (level cap 30).
- City of Dalaran: the Horde quest line brings a Dalaran Sewer Key; it is not
  known yet whether the dungeon needs it, so it is not listed.


2026-10-12 Drop chances (Data\DungeonDropChance.lua)
===============================================================================
- Classic drop chances from the foreverchanges.pro loot tables ("Drop chance
  in Classic", originally Wowhead Classic data). About 90 % of the listed boss
  and trash items have one; new Forever dungeons and items have none. Forever
  drop rates are not published yet.


2026-10-12 Boss creature types (Data\BossInfo.lua)
===============================================================================
- Creature types from the Wowhead Forever NPC tooltips (Classic tooltips as
  fallback); Gahz'rilla and Aku'mai added by hand (Beast). Missing types are
  learned in game (UnitCreatureType) when the boss is targeted.


2026-10-13 First 14 dungeons rebuilt from own sources (OneForAll 5.4.4)
===============================================================================
Hall of Thanes, Ragefire Chasm, Ruins of Lordaeron, Deadmines, Wailing Caverns,
Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps, Excavation Site, Stockade, City of Dalaran,
Gnomeregan, SM Graveyard, Razorfen Kraul and SM Library, generated with
tools/dungeon-gen/early_rebuild.py and early_chains.py:
- Quest givers, turn-ins, objectives, levels, faction: QuestieDB Forever;
  Forever-only quests without QuestieDB data: quest text and "picked up from"
  data on wowforevertalents.com (beta quest cache).
- Quest rewards, XP, money and reputation: wowforevertalents.com quest pages
  (QuestieDB reward items and XP where a page is missing).
- Prerequisite chains: QuestieDB Forever (preQuestSingle / preQuestGroup),
  otherwise the "Comes after" box on wowforevertalents.com.
- Boss loot: wowtbc.gg boss tables plus equipment (green or better) from the
  foreverchanges.pro boss pages; trash loot: wowtbc.gg (rare or better).
  Item names, slots and qualities from the Wowhead tooltip API.
- Boss display IDs: foreverchanges.pro boss pictures.
- Quest start map pins: QuestieDB Forever spawns, otherwise the "picked up
  from" coordinates on wowforevertalents.com. Pins without a source were removed.
- Dungeon entrances: QuestieDB Forever (support/Forever/Zones/dungeons.lua).
- Descriptions, quest notes, boss and item notes: written for OneForAll
  (tools/dungeon-gen/early_texts.py).
